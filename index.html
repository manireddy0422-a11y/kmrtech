Common questions

Short answers, before you pay anything.

Do you apply for jobs on my behalf?

No. We prepare the drafts — you review and send them yourself, from your own email and accounts.

Is a job guaranteed?

No. You pay for training, resume help, interview prep and application support. Hiring is the employer's decision. On the $2,000 training program, if you complete the training and don't get a job, $1,600 is refunded and only $400 is kept.

Do I need a long contract?

No. Start with the $149 pack or $99/month coaching, cancel anytime.

Which technologies do you train?

ServiceNow, Java, SQL, Oracle, Java Full Stack with AI and CyberArk.

Where and when are sessions?

Montreal-based, in English, evenings and weekends. Times are set on your intro call.
